# INSACERMO Discovery Lab V2 — higher-order interaction

This phase asks a stricter blind question: can future-preservation interactions exist at order 3
when **every individual action depends on at most one primitive capability**?

## Exact exhaustive search
Universe:
- 3 worlds
- 3 primitive capabilities
- 5 distinct actions
- every action requirement has cardinality 0 or 1
- 98,280 models enumerated exactly

Among the 29,421 models whose full 3-capability cube has finite cover values:
- third-order Möbius coefficient 0: 29,415
- third-order Möbius coefficient -1: 6

So a nonzero third-order term exists even though no action contains a pairwise or triple
capability requirement.

## Pure 3-way emergent witness
A cleaner six-world construction was then extracted. Its exact profile over the capability cube is

    m(∅)=4
    m({u})=m({v})=m({w})=4
    m({u,v})=m({u,w})=m({v,w})=4
    m({u,v,w})=3

Hence no one capability helps, no pair helps, but all three together reduce the minimum
future-preserving cover from 4 to 3.

Every action is either unconditional or unlocked by exactly one capability.
Therefore the 3-way effect is not encoded as an explicit local AND-condition.
It emerges from the global cover geometry.

One explicit cover construction uses six worlds:
- unconditional covers: {4}, {2}, {0}, {1,3,5}
- u unlocks {0,1}
- v unlocks {2,3}
- w unlocks {4,5}

All proper capability subsets still need four actions; the three pair-covers together cover all
six worlds with three actions.

## Deterministic stress
100,000 additional random models with 4 worlds, 3 capabilities, 7 actions,
again restricting every action requirement to cardinality at most 1.

Finite full-cube profiles: 32,138
Third-order spectrum:
- -2: 1
- -1: 72
- 0: 32,058
- +1: 7

Both signs occur.

## Literature boundary
The alternating third-order coefficient is mathematically classical: it is a Möbius coefficient /
higher-order discrete interaction of a set function. The potentially INSACERMO-specific content is
not the transform itself, but the fact that the future-preservation cost m(C), generated by exact
action-cover feasibility, exhibits higher-order interactions even when the primitive action
activation language itself has interaction order at most 1.

This is currently a computational theorem/witness, not yet a Lean-certified general theorem.
